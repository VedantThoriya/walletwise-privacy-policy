WalletWise Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

WalletWise is a personal finance and expense-tracking application. This policy explains how WalletWise processes information when you use the app.

1. Information We Process

Account information

WalletWise uses Firebase Authentication for Google sign-in. Depending on the sign-in and account synchronization flow, information processed may include an account identifier, email address, display name, and authentication-related timestamps.

SMS and transaction information

When you grant the relevant permissions, WalletWise processes supported financial SMS messages on your device to identify transaction information.

Extracted information may include transaction amount, transaction type, merchant or description, date and time, bank or account-related identifiers, and transaction reference numbers when available.

WalletWise uploads extracted transaction details to its backend for transaction storage and synchronization. The transaction-processing flow is designed to upload extracted details rather than the original SMS text. Transaction information may also be stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Information

WalletWise uses information to authenticate users, detect and organize transactions, synchronize transaction records, provide expense-tracking features, and maintain the application.

AI-powered insights and chatbot features are not active in the app version covered by this policy.

3. Storage and Service Providers

WalletWise uses Firebase Authentication for account authentication, Cloudflare to host its backend API, and Neon to provide PostgreSQL database infrastructure for backend data.

These providers process information as needed to operate the corresponding services. Their own privacy policies may also apply.

4. Data Sharing

Information may be transferred to the service providers listed above to support authentication, backend processing, database storage, and transaction synchronization.

Other third-party SDKs or integrations, if present, must be reviewed and disclosed according to their actual data practices.

5. Permissions and User Choice

WalletWise may request SMS-related or notification access required for transaction detection. You can manage Android permissions through your device settings. Revoking permissions may prevent related features from working.

6. Data Security

WalletWise uses third-party infrastructure to operate its backend and database. We aim to protect information through appropriate technical and organizational measures. No electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

WalletWise may retain account information and transaction records as needed to provide the service.

Account deletion: WalletWise's account-deletion process is still being implemented. This policy does not represent that deletion is currently available.

8. Children's Privacy

WalletWise's intended age group and applicable Google Play target-audience settings should be confirmed before this policy is finalized.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our data practices or app features change. The current version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date when applicable.
