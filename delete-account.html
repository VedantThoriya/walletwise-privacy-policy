WalletWise Account Deletion

This page explains how WalletWise users can request deletion of their accounts and associated data.

How to request deletion

Email hello.localhostlabs@gmail.com from the email address associated with your WalletWise account.

Use the subject line WalletWise Account Deletion Request. Include your WalletWise account email address so that we can identify the account. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, or bank SMS messages.

What data is covered

After verifying the request and implementing the deletion process, the request is intended to cover the user's Firebase Authentication account and associated WalletWise records stored in the backend database.

Transaction records stored locally on a device may require separate removal from that device. Any data retained for a lawful reason, if applicable, will be explained to the user along with the reason and applicable retention period.

Processing requests

We will verify the request, explain any necessary follow-up, and communicate the outcome using the contact email provided. The deletion process and applicable timelines must be confirmed by the WalletWise developer before this page is treated as final.

Contact

For questions about account deletion, contact hello.localhostlabs@gmail.com.
